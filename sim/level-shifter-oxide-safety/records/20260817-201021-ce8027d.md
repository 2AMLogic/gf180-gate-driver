  ### Finding: decision record 0002's thin-oxide claim, re-verified with `XCCOMP` present (issue #155)

  This is the first full-grid re-run of this experiment since
  `design/netlist/level_shifter.spice` gained the `XCCOMP` feedforward

  0007](../../../spec/decision-records/0007-indrv-feedforward-compensation-capacitor.md)).
